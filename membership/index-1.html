Frequently Asked Questions

Understand your membership.

Is creating a KenFolios account free?

Yes. Registration creates your basic KenFolios member account. You may subsequently activate your membership to participate in the wider community.

What is the difference between registration and activation?

Registration creates your account and lets you explore KenFolios. Activation upgrades that account into an active KenFolios Business Community membership and unlocks the core member experience.

What does the subscription add?

Subscription adds an expanding layer of recurring digital services. This can include specialised communities, business tools, lead-generation systems, business management services, mentorship and fundraising platforms, intelligent discovery, premium resources and supported third-party integrations.

Are business tools available to every member?

Business tools and integrations are being introduced beginning with the Subscriber level. Individual tools, services or third-party integrations may have their own availability, eligibility or usage conditions.

Will KenFolios replace the business tools I already use?

Not necessarily. KenFolios can provide its own services while also integrating with external platforms where useful. The objective is to create a connected business ecosystem rather than force every member into a single technology stack.

Which external platforms can KenFolios integrate with?

The integration layer may progressively support relevant communication, productivity, marketing, collaboration and business platforms such as WhatsApp, Telegram, Google, Meta, LinkedIn, Slack, Discord, Wix and other services. Availability will depend on the specific integration and supported platform capabilities.

What happens if I stop subscribing?

Ending a subscription does not automatically erase your activated membership. Subscriber-only tools, integrations and benefits may stop, while benefits attached to your activated membership remain subject to the applicable membership terms.

Are all groups available to every member?

Different groups may have different eligibility rules. Some may be available to activated members, while specialised groups and services may require an active subscription or other eligibility.

Are online and offline meetups part of KenFolios?

Yes. KenFolios uses both online and offline formats to help members interact. Individual events may have their own eligibility, registration requirements or ticket pricing.

How does member support work?

Standard membership support is designed to be digital-first. KenFolios may provide support through web forms, social channels, WhatsApp and automated support systems as the infrastructure evolves.

Can I directly purchase Founder Circle membership?

Founder Circle is intended to be invitation-based. Members first participate through the standard KenFolios membership journey before becoming eligible for consideration.

Is VStreet automatically included with KenFolios membership?

No. VStreet is being positioned as a separate, highly curated relationship layer. Participation and selection are distinct from standard KenFolios membership.

Will KenFolios introduce more tools and membership benefits?

Yes. The ecosystem is continuously being developed. New digital features, business tools, integrations, groups, events and services may be introduced over time.
