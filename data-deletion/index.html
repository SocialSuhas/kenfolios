Data Deletion & Privacy Requests

KenFolios Media Private Limited
Last updated: 3 November 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of personal information held by KenFolios, including information associated with our websites, business community, membership accounts, support services and WhatsApp Business Platform communications.

1. How to Submit a Request

Submit a request through our Support page, or send a direct message to KenFolios through our official Instagram account linked from our website. Please clearly write “Personal Data Deletion Request”. For privacy, do not post personal information in public comments.

2. Information to Include

Please provide your full name, the email address registered with KenFolios, the relevant WhatsApp/mobile number if applicable, the data or account you want deleted, and a short description of your request. You may explain your reason, but giving a reason is not mandatory to exercise an applicable deletion right. If you use a different contact email, please mention your registered email for account matching. Never share passwords, OTPs, card numbers, or identity documents in an initial request.

3. Verification and Processing

We may contact you privately to verify that you are the account holder or otherwise authorized to make the request. We will review the request, explain any additional information needed, and respond within the time required by applicable law. Where deletion is appropriate, we will delete or de-identify eligible information from relevant systems and instruct applicable processors where required and feasible. Some backup copies may remain temporarily until routine expiry, subject to safeguards.

4. Information That May Be Retained

Deletion is subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements. Certain payment, tax, invoicing, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or regulatory records may need to be retained for legally required periods. We will not treat a deletion request as permission to erase records that must legally be preserved.

5. WhatsApp Messaging Preferences

If you only wish to stop promotional WhatsApp messages, please state that in your support request or use the available opt-out instructions in messages. Opting out of marketing is distinct from deleting a KenFolios account. Essential service notifications may be handled separately where legally permitted. KenFolios may process messaging identifiers, delivery/read status, and support conversations as described in our Privacy Policy.

6. Other Privacy Rights

You may also request access, correction, or withdrawal of consent where applicable. Rights and exceptions vary by jurisdiction. We will handle requests under applicable Indian data protection law and, where relevant, other applicable privacy laws.

7. Contact and Related Policies

Use kenfolios.com/support/ or the official KenFolios Instagram account for requests. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for additional information.
