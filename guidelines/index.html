KenFolios Business Community

Community Guidelines

Build relationships. Create opportunities. Respect the community. These guidelines explain the standards that help KenFolios remain professional, useful, trusted and welcoming for everyone.

Our approach Professional freedom with responsible participation.

KenFolios encourages conversations, collaboration, business development, networking and new opportunities — while protecting members from spam, harassment, misuse of data and deceptive behaviour.

KenFolios Business Community brings together entrepreneurs, founders, professionals, creators, investors, mentors and business leaders across digital communities, events, programs, tools and partner platforms. These Guidelines apply wherever members participate in an activity associated with KenFolios.

The simple rule
If an activity would make other members less comfortable introducing themselves, sharing their professional information, attending an event or participating in a group, don't do it.
01

Be professional and respectful

Strong opinions and disagreements are part of business communities. Personal attacks are not. Treat other members with the same professionalism you would expect in a serious workplace or business meeting.

  • No harassment, threats, intimidation or abusive conduct.
  • No discriminatory or degrading remarks directed at individuals or groups.
  • No persistent unwanted personal communication.
  • No deliberately disruptive behaviour in groups, events or community spaces.
02

Build relationships before broadcasting promotions

KenFolios is a business network, so members may introduce their work, seek opportunities and respond to relevant requirements. But community access should not be treated as unlimited advertising inventory.

Good participation

Responding to a member seeking a supplier, posting a relevant opportunity, sharing expertise or making a useful introduction.

Avoid

Repeatedly posting the same advertisement, flooding multiple groups, or forcing unrelated conversations toward your offer.

03

No spam, scraping or unsolicited mass outreach

Access to a KenFolios group, directory, event or platform does not automatically give permission to contact every member for commercial purposes.

  • Do not scrape, copy or harvest member contact details.
  • Do not send bulk WhatsApp messages, emails, calls or DMs using community data without permission.
  • Do not add members to external groups, mailing lists or databases without consent.
  • Do not automate member outreach in ways that bypass community rules or platform policies.
A relevant one-to-one introduction can be valuable. Unrequested mass prospecting can quickly make a community unusable.
04

Protect member privacy and community data

Information visible inside KenFolios should be used only for legitimate participation and in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

  • Do not redistribute private conversations or member information without permission.
  • Do not publish screenshots of private groups or communications in a way that exposes members unnecessarily.
  • Do not sell, license, export or commercially exploit member data.
  • Respect requests from other members to stop contacting them.
05

Represent yourself and your work truthfully

Trust is essential in a business network. Your identity, organisation, professional experience, opportunities and commercial claims should not intentionally mislead other members.

  • No impersonation or intentionally false identities.
  • No fabricated credentials, testimonials or business claims.
  • No deceptive investment, employment, fundraising or commercial offers.
  • No fraudulent links, payment requests, schemes or misrepresentation.
06

Conduct business independently and do your own due diligence

KenFolios helps people discover and connect with one another. A member's presence in the community does not by itself mean KenFolios guarantees, endorses or verifies every statement, product, service, investment or transaction.

  • Verify counterparties before making payments or commitments.
  • Use written agreements where appropriate.
  • Evaluate investment and fundraising opportunities independently.
  • Do not imply KenFolios endorsement unless you have written authorisation.
07

Keep groups useful and relevant

KenFolios operates city, industry, interest, local business and other focused communities. Members should respect the purpose of each space.

  • Post information relevant to the group wherever possible.
  • Avoid excessive forwards, repetitive greetings and unrelated promotional material.
  • Follow group-specific instructions issued by authorised admins or moderators.
  • Use the most appropriate group rather than posting the same message everywhere.
08

Events are professional community spaces too

These standards also apply at KenFolios online meetings, offline networking events, founder sessions, workshops, meetups and other community activities.

  • Respect organisers, speakers, attendees and venue rules.
  • Do not harass attendees or misuse attendee lists after an event.
  • Do not misrepresent an unofficial gathering as an official KenFolios event.
  • Follow event-specific safety, registration and participation requirements.
09

Member-led groups and events carry responsibility

KenFolios may allow members to create groups, organise activities or participate in community-led initiatives. That freedom should be used responsibly.

  • Use the KenFolios name, logo and brand only in the manner authorised for the activity.
  • Clearly distinguish member-led activities from activities directly organised by KenFolios where relevant.
  • Do not collect money, commitments or personal data in KenFolios' name without appropriate authorisation.
  • Organisers are responsible for maintaining professional conduct in the spaces they manage.
10

Share lawful, responsible and useful content

Members are responsible for what they publish or distribute through the community.

  • No unlawful, fraudulent, malicious or intentionally deceptive content.
  • No content that infringes another person's privacy, intellectual property or confidential information.
  • No malware, phishing links or intentionally harmful files.
  • Do not use community spaces primarily to provoke, inflame or derail professional discussions.
11

Don't exploit community access

Membership is intended for participation in the ecosystem, not for extracting the ecosystem itself.

  • Do not build competing databases by copying KenFolios member information.
  • Do not resell access to KenFolios groups, resources or member information.
  • Do not reproduce internal resources or restricted community material without permission.
  • Do not use technical methods intended to bypass membership, moderation or access controls.
12

Report serious problems instead of escalating them publicly

If you encounter suspected fraud, harassment, threats, data misuse, impersonation or another serious violation, report it to KenFolios through the available support or administrative channels.

Public accusations, doxxing or prolonged disputes inside community groups can create additional harm. KenFolios may ask members to provide evidence privately so the matter can be reviewed appropriately.

13

How these Guidelines are enforced

KenFolios may take action based on the nature, seriousness, context and frequency of a violation. Not every issue requires the same response.

Reminder or warning For minor or first-time issues where corrective action is appropriate.
Restrictions Content removal, posting limits or removal from particular groups or activities.
Suspension or removal For serious, repeated, fraudulent, abusive or harmful conduct.
Serious issues involving fraud, threats, harassment, unlawful activity, deliberate data misuse or attempts to compromise community systems may result in immediate action without a prior warning.
14

Context matters

These Guidelines cannot predict every situation. KenFolios may consider context, intent, impact, member safety, previous conduct and applicable law when reviewing behaviour. Moderation decisions may therefore differ between situations that appear similar at first glance.

15

Guidelines may evolve with the community

KenFolios continues to add groups, events, tools, memberships, integrations and new ways for members to interact. These Guidelines may be updated as the ecosystem evolves. Material policy changes may also be reflected in the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy or other applicable rules.

These Guidelines work together with our legal policies.

The Community Guidelines are intended to explain expected behaviour in practical language. They do not replace the KenFolios Terms of Service or Privacy Policy. If there is a conflict, the applicable legal terms and policies govern.
